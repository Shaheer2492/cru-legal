Privacy Policy for Cru

Last Updated: January 3, 2026

Introduction

Cru ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

When you register for and use the App, we collect the following personal information:

  • Account Information: Email address, phone number, password (encrypted)
  • Profile Information: Display name, legal first and last name, date of birth, gender, bio, ethnicity, nationality
  • Photos: Profile pictures (up to 3 images)
  • Verification Documents: Government-issued ID photos (front and back) for age verification
  • Location Data: Precise location when using the app to discover nearby events
  • User Content: Event details you create, chat messages, event attendance history
  • Connections: List of users you've connected with through the app

Information Automatically Collected

When you use our App, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features accessed, time spent on features, app crashes
  • Analytics: Event views, event joins, user interactions (collected via Firebase Analytics)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  1. Provide Services: Create and manage your account, enable you to create and join events, facilitate group chats
  2. Verification: Verify your age and identity for safety purposes
  3. Location Features: Show you events near your current location
  4. Personalization: Filter events based on your age, gender, and preferences
  5. Safety & Security: Monitor for violations of our Terms of Service, prevent fraud and abuse
  6. Communications: Send you notifications about events, account updates, and app features
  7. Analytics: Understand how users interact with the app to improve our services
  8. Legal Compliance: Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Information Shared With Other Users

  • Public Profile: Your display name, age, gender, profile photos, bio, interests, ethnicity, and nationality are visible to other users
  • Event Information: When you join an event, other attendees can see your profile
  • Group Chats: Messages you send in event group chats are visible to all event participants
  • Connections: Your connections list is private and not visible to other users

Third-Party Service Providers

We share your information with trusted third-party service providers:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud Platform): Hosts our database, authentication, storage, and analytics
    Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: If you sign in with Google
  • Apple Sign-In: If you sign in with Apple

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS
  • Firebase Security: Data stored using Firebase's secure infrastructure with access controls
  • Password Protection: Passwords are hashed and encrypted (we never store plain-text passwords)

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct your profile information through the app settings
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting support@cruapp.com
  • Opt-Out of Location: Disable location services in your device settings

Children's Privacy

Cru is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.
